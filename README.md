#Tea Shop 

- The Tea Shop is an online shopping platform for consumers to order tea from online. They can browser through a selection of teas that are offered, then selecting the tea they like, and purchasing the item. It is later shipped to their door. This is created for people who are too busy to pick their drink in person.
